Our Story

Founded with a heartfelt passion for hospitality, Wolban Short Lets was born from a simple mission: to offer guests a warm, inviting atmosphere where they can feel completely at home — even while travelling.

From our character-rich Vintage apartments to our sleek, modern Contemporary spaces, every room is thoughtfully designed to offer not just a place to sleep, but a genuine experience.

Why Choose Us?

  • High-quality, fully furnished and professionally maintained apartments
  • Friendly and responsive customer service — before, during and after your stay
  • Flexible short-term rental periods tailored to your schedule
  • Prime Ikeja location — close to the airport, business districts and amenities
  • Secure, well-lit premises with 24/7 support available
